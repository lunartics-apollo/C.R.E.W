(01) Mission briefing

C.R.E.W puts you in charge of a space mission. Design, test and launch your own CubeSat, a satellite the size of a shoebox, as Sarawak’s first space mission.

A pixel-art mission game. Take a call from the Premier of Sarawak, join your team at Mission Control, then build the satellite part by part. Every choice, from the camera and the battery to the radio and the orbit, decides how much usable data your CubeSat brings home. When things go wrong, the debrief shows you exactly why.
